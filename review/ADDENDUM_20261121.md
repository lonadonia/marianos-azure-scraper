## 1. What Darren's review found, and what 1.10.0rc6 did with it (OR-25)

R7's coverage file (the copy finalized offline, `evidence/runs/R7fin_copy/export/coverage_reconciliation.csv`)
reports Dog Food 27017 and Cat Treats 27022 **DONE, gap 0**. Their counted pages list 224 and 52 distinct products;
R7 kept a record of 200 and 32. The 44 others (24 on Dog Food's page 7, 20 on Cat Treats' page 2) are in no record:
the two pages OR-24 emptied. G3 called both listings incomplete, so R7's gates failed and its publication was refused
(exit 6), as 1.10.0rc6 reported. But the coverage file and G3 disagreed, and neither verdict looked at the records:

* the coverage file took the larger of the identities on the counted pages and the listing's own count, with the
  listing's own status;
* G3 took the listing's own count of distinct products seen against the source's total;
* the listing's own status (DONE) came from the pages' identities reaching the total, never from the records.

A loss smaller than the G3 thresholds (warn below 0.98, fail below 0.90) therefore passed every check. The test
added for 1.10.0rc7 shows it on 1.10.0rc6: one product of 61 left without a record on a counted page was validated
and **published**, the automatic publication replacing the previous good dataset (exit 0), while the coverage file
said DONE, gap 0. A whole page lost (R7's shape) was refused by G3 (coverage 0.607, exit 6) while the coverage file
said DONE, 61 unique, gap 0.
