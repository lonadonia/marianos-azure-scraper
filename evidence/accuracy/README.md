## The four product pages (digital coupons)

What a product page can show, and what is said from it: a **coupon shown** (the page was rendered and loaded, and a
Savings row whose own qualifying products name the UPC); an **offer for other products** (a Savings row that does
not name the UPC); **no savings shown** (the provider states it ran the page's scripts, the page loaded, and it has
no Savings row: no offer on that page at that time); and **unread** (the page loading, the scripts not run, or no
page read): nothing is learnt, and the record says the coupon is unknown, never absent. An expiry is stated as
displayed, and an offer is called expired only from its own displayed date.

- **0002310014848** (Cat Treats 27022): its card on page 2 of R7 at 03:02:44Z on 2026-11-17, its own page read at 03:02:58Z (HTTP 200; the provider states it ran the page's scripts). The page had loaded and showed no Savings section: **no savings shown**, no offer on that page at that time (as on 2026-11-06). R7 kept no record of it: 20 of that page's 24 products, this one among them, are in no record of the run, although the page was counted (OR-24).
- **0003980014995** (Hardware Home Improvement 33): in R7 its card on page 2 (asked again after page 2 had repeated page 1), its own page read at 04:01:59Z: the provider did not run the page's scripts (unread). Read again in the pilot P8 (its card on page 2 at 04:21:49Z, its page at 04:21:54Z, HTTP 200): the scripts ran, the page had loaded and showed no Savings section: **no savings shown**, no offer on that page at that time. This is its first rendered read: on 2026-11-06 it was never brought rendered. Its record is in R7's export (its card read at 04:01:55Z), flagged `DIGITAL_COUPONS_UNOBSERVED` (unknown, not absent).
- **0001780014939** and **0001780014941** (Dog Food 27017): both cards on page 7; their own pages read in R7 at 04:01:41Z and 04:01:44Z and again in P8 at 04:22:40Z and 04:22:42Z (HTTP 200 each time). not when the coupon needs several units or its complete terms were not read (`assembly/offers.py`,
