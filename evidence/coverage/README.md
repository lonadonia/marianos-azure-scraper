## Listings above the site's window

The site's search lets a reader reach 250 results of a listing. A listing that reports more is read through the
subdivisions its own page names (child listings), or through the retailer's brand filter. Every page answer counted
for them is checked for store (location 53100502, fulfillment PICKUP, the collector's store check PASS), page (the
page answered is the page asked, of the listing asked) and filter (the filter asked is the filter the page states),
and the union of the distinct products of the listing and its subdivisions is reconciled with the listing's reported
total, never a sum of counts ([`BRANCHES_R7.csv`](BRANCHES_R7.csv), page by page in
[`BRANCH_PAGES_R7.csv`](BRANCH_PAGES_R7.csv), [`../tools/branches6.py`](../tools/branches6.py)).

R7: 4 listings above the window, through 11 subdivisions; reconciled (union
equal to the reported total): 0; by the collector's status: INCOMPLETE 2, NOT_COLLECTED 2. Pages checked
45, failing a check 0; listings where the union disagrees with the collector's
own status: none. Products the reported totals name that the run did not read: 889.

## What the statuses mean

**DONE**: the listing's distinct products reach the total the site reported for it (for a listing above the
window, every subdivision DONE or EMPTY and the union of distinct products reaching the reported total). **EMPTY**:
the site reports 0 results. **INCOMPLETE**: read, but not to its total (the site's repeated pages outlasted the
re-asks, a page whose re-asks were all answered with an earlier page, or a subdivision incomplete). **NOT_COLLECTED**:
planned, and not read to an end by this run. A run is judged on its own: no other run's listings are added to R7's
counts.
