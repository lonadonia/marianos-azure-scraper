# Operations: lanes, stops, a hard kill, resumption and refusals (1.10.0rc6)

All on Hamza's VM, live, through the gate of the credit's own ledger: every submission is reserved before it is
sent, a stage's executions share one account (two lanes draw on the same reservations and the same ceiling), and the
gate lets at most as many requests be in flight as the stage has lanes ([`../ledger/`](../ledger/README.md)). The
checks ran on the candidate 1.10.0rc6 during the pilot P7 (one lane) and the slices of the full run R7 (two lanes).

**How "not asked again" is checked** ([`../tools/no_refetch.py`](../tools/no_refetch.py), on the run's own
`listing_pages` rows, every segment): a request is its listing, partition, route, page and filter. After a
request's completed row, any later row of the same request is a re-fetch, unless the run set that request aside
(C1) and the set-aside request itself names that very row among its re-asks (`deferred_requests.attempts`, by
listing-page id). That happens by design for a listing's first page above the 250-result window that names no child
listing and no brand: it is counted, then asked again for its filters. R7 over all its slices:
[`NO_REFETCH_R7.txt`](NO_REFETCH_R7.txt).

**Requests in flight** ([`PEAK_IN_FLIGHT.txt`](PEAK_IN_FLIGHT.txt), [`../tools/peak_in_flight.py`](../tools/peak_in_flight.py)):
from each stage's ledger rows (reserved, dispatched, settled or left unknown, with their times), the most requests
in flight at once per stage and per lease, and the outcomes left unknown at the stage's end.

## One lane and two lanes on 1.10.0rc6

P7 (one lane, rows 0 and 27, a pilot) and R7 (two lanes, the whole list) do not read the same scope, so their
rates are not compared here; each is reported for what it is: P7, one lane: 9 submissions in 17.5 minutes of activity (one segment, 23:20:30Z to 23:37:57Z) for 7 page answers, 2 of them counted (4 repeated page 1 and waited for their re-asks; 1 was malformed and asked again), never more than one request in flight ([`../runs/P7/RUN_REPORT.md`](../runs/P7/RUN_REPORT.md)). R7, two lanes: 256 submissions in 53.8 minutes of activity over ten executions (2026-11-17, 03:00:20Z to 05:35:38Z) for 216 page answers, 132 of them counted (1.939 submissions and 24.4 s of activity a counted page); at most 2 requests in flight in every slice, both lanes under one ledger lease ([`PEAK_IN_FLIGHT.txt`](PEAK_IN_FLIGHT.txt)). With two lanes R7 lost the records of two counted pages while their named product pages were read (OR-24, corrected on the branch `marianos-1.10-next`, not released).

What 1.10.0rc6 changed for the lanes: a listing read through its subdivisions is finished before the plan's next
listing is started (the task queue orders listing tasks by their place in the plan: depth first), and a subdivision
waiting for a re-ask holds no lane (`db/queue.py`, commit `b75201b`). The comparison of one and two lanes on the same
scope (rows 22 to 27, L1 one lane and L2 two lanes) was measured on 1.10.0rc4, before that change; it is complete at
the tag [`review-1.10.0rc5-20261116`](https://github.com/lonadonia/marianos-azure-scraper/tree/review-1.10.0rc5-20261116/evidence/operations)
(`evidence/operations/LANES_L1_L2.json`) and is an earlier candidate's measurement, not one of 1.10.0rc6.

## The site's repeated pages, by hour

The site sometimes answers a page 2 or later with page 1 again. C1 sets such a request aside and asks it again,
at most twice, each at least 300 s after the previous answer; a page whose re-asks are all answered with an earlier
page stays incomplete. Over every live stage of 1.10.0rc6 (P7, R6 and R7), by hour (UTC):
[`REPEATED_PAGES_BY_HOUR_RC6.csv`](REPEATED_PAGES_BY_HOUR_RC6.csv) (answers to a page 2 or later, new or repeated)
and [`REASK_OUTCOMES_BY_HOUR_RC6.csv`](REASK_OUTCOMES_BY_HOUR_RC6.csv) (requests set aside: recovered at the first
or second re-ask, exhausted, waiting). R7, over its five slices: of its answers to a page 2 or later, 7 of 16 repeated page 1 at 03Z (44%), 28 of 89 at 04Z (31%) and 17 of 34 at 05Z (50%). Of the 35 requests it set aside (33 repeated pages, 2 pages without the facets needed to subdivide), 23 were recovered by a re-ask (14 at the first, 9 at the second), 6 had both re-asks repeated (they leave Dog Treats 27018, Light Bulbs 26002, Champagne & Sparkling Wine 08180, Hard Seltzers 08183 and one subdivision each of Red Wine 08120 and White Wine 08122 incomplete) and 6 were still waiting when R7 stopped. The watcher's repeat rule (a quarter or more of a slice's answers to a page 2 or later repeated) ended R7a, R7a2 and R7b's last execution. The same measure over every run of 2026-11-16, which set R7's
start at 03Z (10% then), is in [`../estimate/`](../estimate/README.md).
